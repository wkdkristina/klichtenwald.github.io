# Case Study: KPI & Dashboard Reporting
## Project Objective

Apex Industrial was suffering from "Index Bloat." With 50,000 URLs discovered but only a fraction indexed, Google was wasting crawl budget on near-duplicate variants. I implemented a Zero Waste SEO Architecture to consolidate equity and harden technical infrastructure.

## The Execution Strategy
<ul>
    <li><b>Profit First Audit:</b> Using a weighted matrix, I prioritized high-converting product categories for immedate consolidation.</li>
    <li><b>Quick Win Implementation:</b> By converting 30% of the catalog into Parent-Child URL structures, we reclaimed massive crawl budget within the first 30 days.</li>
    <li><b>AEO Ready Schema:</b> I deployed technicalSpecs and FAQ schema to provide the granular data points required for modern Answer Engines.</li>
</ul>

<p>Priority Score is calculted as follows:</br>
(Conversion Rate * 2) + Effort Score </br>
Effort Scoring is based on a 1-10 scale. 10 is "Seamless/Easy" and 1 is "Extremely Resource Intensive".</p>
<ul>
    <li><b>10-8 (Quick Wins):</b> Tasks I am able to do in the CMS or GSC. (i.e. Requesting re-indexing, updating a Meta Description, or Canonicalizing a few pages.)</li>
    <li><b>7-5 (Moderate):</b> Tasks that require a bulk upload or a basic script (i.e. Mass-implementing technicalSpecs schema via GTM or a CSV import.)</li>
    <li><b>4-2 (Hard/Resource Intensive):</b> Tasks that require a Developer, a Site Migration, or a significant URL architecture overhaul.</li>
    <li><b>1 (Heavy Lift):</b> Redesigning the entire backend database or a server-side change that requires months of coordination.</li>
</ul>

<p> I use a 1-10 ease of implementation scale to ensure we aren't just chasing high-revenue items that take six months to build. By weighing the effort, I can identify the high-impact, low-friction moves that stabilize the client's traffic immediately while we prepare for the longer, more complex architectural shifts.</p>
